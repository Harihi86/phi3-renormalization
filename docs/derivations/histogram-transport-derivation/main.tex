\section*{An exact finite representation}
Each of the seven coordinates has \(B=16\) equal cells in the original
coordinate \(x\), with positive masses
\[
 m_j=\frac{n_j}{N},\qquad N=65536,\quad n_j\in\mathbb Z_{>0},
 \qquad \sum_{j=0}^{B-1}n_j=N.
\]
Set \(c_0=0\), \(c_{j+1}=c_j+m_j\).
For \(c_j\le y<c_{j+1}\), define
\[
 x(y)=\frac jB+\frac{N}{Bn_j}(y-c_j),
 \qquad J(y)=\frac{N}{Bn_j}.
\]
Cell endpoints match exactly:
\(x(c_j)=j/B\), \(x(c_{j+1})=(j+1)/B\).
Each slope is strictly positive and every original cell is covered once.
Hence for any integrable \(f\),
\[
 \int_0^1 f(x(y))J(y)\,\mathrm dy
 =\sum_j\int_{j/B}^{(j+1)/B}f(x)\,\mathrm dx
 =\int_0^1 f(x)\,\mathrm dx.
\]
For seven coordinates the transformed density is
\[
 \widetilde f(y_1,\ldots,y_7)
 =f(x_1(y_1),\ldots,x_7(y_7))\prod_{a=1}^7J_a(y_a).
\]
The same substitution preserves the absolute integral. No domain tail is
discarded and no graph coefficient is altered. At an internal boundary the
implementation consistently chooses the right cell. Arithmetic that cannot
classify a cell is rejected.

\section*{Training and validation are distinct}
The completed soft-kernel diagnostic supplies 128 authenticated requests
and 32768 stored nodes. Repeats zero and one fit candidate histograms;
all fourteen models are saved and hash-sealed before repeats two and three
are read. The candidate family contains uniform maps and 4, 8 or 16 cells
with uniform mixtures of \(1/10\) or \(1/4\).
Fitted masses are quantized to positive integer units and normalized exactly.

The holdout comparison uses the second-moment expression
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_{\rho}(f/\rho)
 =\int\frac{f(x)^2}{\rho(x)}\,\mathrm dx
     -\left(\int f(x)\,\mathrm dx\right)^2.
\]
Its estimates select a prospective method; they do not establish precision.
The frozen models must pass native mapping checks and fresh fixed diagnostics
before independent production is considered. Production and stored replay
retain the existing acceptance gates.

\section*{Independent checks}
Exact rational checks verify normalization, every cell endpoint and polynomial
moments through degree four. Native map tests cover all 210 internal
coordinate boundaries across the two models, at the boundary and on both
sides, plus a base point for each model: 632 points and 5056 scalar interval
checks. The reference computes the map with rational arithmetic.

Full graph checks then use the independent original-routing reference:
formal Taylor products, conditional rotation frames and direct angular
integrals. These checks include the complete Jacobian and do not substitute
numerical proximity for the original exact forest identities. Source hashes,
actual exits and precision results are stored in the recovery checkpoint.
