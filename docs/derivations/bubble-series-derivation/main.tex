It does not change graph symmetry factors, counterterm signs, external momentum

\section{A positive series for the bubble}
Let
\[
 B(t)=\frac{t^2}{60}\,{}_2F_1(1,1;7/2;-t/4),\qquad t\geq0 .
\]
With \(x=t/(t+4)\), the transformed series is
\[
 B(t)=\frac{t^2}{3(t+4)}
       \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{2n+5}.
\]
The transformed hypergeometric coefficient \(c_n=5/(2n+5)\) is fixed
by \(c_0=1\) and
\(c_{n+1}/c_n=(n+5/2)/(n+7/2)\).
The positive tail after \(N\) terms obeys
\[
 0\leq \sum_{n=N}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{2n+c}
 \leq \frac{x^N}{(2N+c)(1-x)},\quad 0\leq x<1 .
\]
For large \(t\), put
\[
 a=\frac{t}{2(\sqrt{1+t/4}+1)},\qquad
 t=a(a+4),\qquad q(t)=\sqrt{\frac{t}{t+4}}.
\]
Then
\(\operatorname{atanh}q(t)=2\operatorname{atanh}q(a)\).
Repeated halving brings the series argument \(q(a)^2\) below \(1/4\).
The reconstruction uses
\[
 B(t)=\frac{(t+4)^{3/2}}{3\sqrt t}\operatorname{atanh}q(t)
      -\frac{4t}{9}-\frac43.
\]
For small \(t\), the direct positive series avoids this subtraction.

\section{A squared-bubble primitive with an exact recurrence}
Define
\[
 A(t)=\int_0^t\frac{B(s)^2}{(1+s)^3}\,ds,\qquad
 A(t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_n t^{n+5},\qquad a_0=\frac1{18000}.
\]
The bubble satisfies
\[
 t^2(t+4)B''-t(t+2)B'+(t-4)B=0.
\]
Set \(p=-(t+2)/(t(t+4))\), \(r=(t-4)/(t^2(t+4))\), and \(Y=B^2\).
Direct differentiation gives the symmetric-square equation
\[
 Y'''+3pY''+(2p^2+p'+4r)Y'+(4pr+2r')Y=0.
\]
Substitution \(Y=(1+t)^3 A'\), clearing denominators, and matching
powers of \(t\) yields a finite recurrence over exact rational numbers.
The implementation checks this operator algebraically and compares its
first 24 coefficients with an independent Cauchy convolution.

In detail, writing \(B(t)=t^2\sum_{n\geq0}b_n t^n\) gives
\[
 b_0=\frac1{60},\qquad
 b_{n+1}=-\frac{n+1}{4n+14}\,b_n.
\]
The independent coefficient construction is
\[
 a_n=\frac1{n+5}
 \sum_{\substack{i,j,k\geq0\\i+j+k=n}}
 b_i b_j(-1)^k\binom{k+2}{2}.
\]
Since \(\lvert b_n\rvert\leq 4^{-n}/60\),
\[
 |a_n|\leq\frac{\binom{n+2}{2}}{2025(n+5)}
          \leq\frac{n+2}{4050}.
\]
Consequently, for \(0\leq t<1\),
\[
 \left|A(t)-\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}a_n t^{n+5}\right|
 \leq
 \frac{t^{N+5}\bigl((N+2)-(N+1)t\bigr)}
      {4050(1-t)^2}.
\]
The Euler integral for the hypergeometric function also gives
\[
 B(s)\geq\frac{s^2}{60(1+s/4)},\qquad
 A(t)\geq\frac{t^5}{18000(1+t/4)^2(1+t)^3}.
\]
The implemented series uses \(0\leq t\leq1/16\), a bounded term count,
and the ratio of these two bounds to choose its truncation.

\section{Verification scope}
All recurrence and coefficient statements above use exact rational algebra.
The tail estimates bound truncation in exact real arithmetic. They do not
enclose floating point rounding. The implementation is an independent
high precision reference, not an interval certificate for a graph integral.

The positive bubble series has six independent 640-digit reference checks.
For \(t\in[34,34+1/200]\), direct series quadrature of
\(B(t)/(1+t)^2\) and \(B(t)^2/(1+t)^3\), with Gauss--Legendre orders 96 and
192, agrees with independently derived primitives within a relative
\(10^{-600}\). These four scalar cross-checks completed in approximately
34 seconds. Quadrature-order agreement is not a rigorous quadrature bound.

The squared-primitive series is tested separately against the existing
dilogarithm/trilogarithm primitive, with extra precision to compensate for
its subtraction at \(t=0\), and against direct scalar quadrature.
See the saved job receipts for actual completion and source hashes.
No running verifier is modified, no old failed job is relabelled successful,
and none of these scalar tests increases the accepted graph count.
