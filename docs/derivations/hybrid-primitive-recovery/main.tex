\noindent This note describes the new scalar evaluation stage for
$L4E3G39$ and $L4E3G394$. It retains their sixteen original BPH forest
states, all $720$ Hepp orders, exact rational coefficient collection,
the original external Gram matrix and both independent determinant constructions.
It is not a new whole-integral precision certificate.

\paragraph{Normalization identity.}
Write $A_2(t)=P(w(t))-P(1)$, with
\[
 w(t)=\frac{4}{(\sqrt{t+4}+\sqrt t)^2}, \qquad t>0 .
\]
After exact collection of equal arguments the forest contribution has the
form $F=\sum_j c_j P(w(t_j))$, where $c_j\in\mathbb Q$ and
$\sum_j c_j=0$. For the set $S=\{j:t_j\leq1/16\}$,
\[
 F=\sum_{j\in S}c_j A_2(t_j)
   +\sum_{j\notin S}c_jP(w(t_j))
   +\left(\sum_{j\in S}c_j\right)P(1).
\]
The final coefficient is collected exactly before evaluating the constant.
The identity $\sum_jc_j=0$ does not imply
$\sum_{j\in S}c_j=0$. Omitting or reversing this term is an error.
The new preflight includes two nonzero-normalization cases and
rejects both errors against independent closed-form references.

\paragraph{Small argument.}
For $0<t\leq1/16$, the normalized series is evaluated directly:
\[
 A_2(t)=t^5\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n t^n,\qquad c_0=\frac1{18000}.
\]
For a truncation after $n=N-1$, the implemented tail bound is
\[
 |R_N(t)|\leq
 \frac{t^{N+5}\big((N+2)-(N+1)t\big)}{4050(1-t)^2}.
\]
The bound and a positive lower bound
\[
 A_2(t)\geq
 \frac{t^5}{18000(1+t/4)^2(1+t)^3}
\]
are checked with interval arithmetic to choose the truncation.
The coefficient recurrence and Horner operations also use interval arithmetic.

\paragraph{Large argument.}
Outside the small-argument branch, a six-periodic real series is used
only when interval arithmetic proves $w\leq1/4$; otherwise the original
closed form is retained. Let $L=\log w$ and $m=N+1$. The real-series
remainder obeys
\[
 |R_N(w)|\leq\frac{w^{N+1}}{1-w}
 \left[
 \frac{M_0}{m}
 +M_1\left(\frac{|L|}{m}+\frac1{m^2}\right)
 +M_2\left(\frac{|L|^2}{m}+\frac{2|L|}{m^2}+\frac2{m^3}\right)
 \right].
\]
Here $M_k$ are the certified maxima of the six-periodic coefficients
for the corresponding logarithmic components.
This bound is added to the computed interval.
The complete forest output must retain at least $80$ relative bits;
working precision may increase up to $8192$ bits.

\paragraph{Independent checks.}
The successful whole-graph preflight verifies $23\,760$ exact mapping
branches, $20$ coefficient comparisons against freshly constructed,
unreduced $720$-order sums, and $20$ native intervals against $10$
independent closed references. It rejects $29$ injected errors.
Transport and stored replay tests independently reconstruct the
beta maps from binomial tails and the Jacobian from factorial ratios.
They do not import the production transport evaluator.

\paragraph{Lossless diagnostic recovery.}
The original diagnostic actually exited after timeouts.
Independent replay validated $18$ complete groups, consisting of
$36$ requests and $9\,216$ nodes. The remaining $14$ groups are
recomputed at all $512$ original nodes per group, using $64$-point
sequential requests. The fixed plan remains $32$ groups and
$16\,384$ nodes with seeds $12410244001+239r$, $r=0,1,2,3$.
No partial failed group is selected for reuse and no statistical samples
are added. Selection is permitted only after the complete fixed plan.
Fresh production, if selected, retains the unused seed base
$12510244001$ and four $8192$-node repeats per kernel.
All statistical gates and independent whole-integral audit requirements
remain unchanged.
