\section*{Original forests and retained propagators}
Each graph has three disjoint primitive children, hence eight
forests with sign \((-1)^{|F|}\). The bubble retains its degree-two
Taylor variable; each triangle uses degree zero. All original
Gaussian, positive-polynomial and Schur-complement constructions
of \(U,F\) are compared exactly.

Write
\[
 B(t)=\int_0^1[(1+x(1-x)t)\log(1+x(1-x)t)-x(1-x)t]\,\mathrm dx,
 \quad J(t)=\frac{B(t)}{1+t}.
\]
Then \(B(0)=B'(0)=0\), recording both mass and kinetic subtraction.
Of the four original host propagators, precisely one matching
the bubble boundary is absorbed into \(J\). The other three remain.
Each triangle is \(T_i=-L(a_i,b_i,1)\), including simplex volume
\(1/2\). At zero physical momentum, \(T_i=-E(t)/2\), where
\[
 E(t)=2\sqrt{\frac{t+4}{t}}\operatorname{arsinh}\frac{\sqrt t}{2}-2,
 \qquad B(t)=\frac{t+4}{6}E(t)-\frac t9.
\]
Thus the complete product is \(HJT_1T_2\), with baseline
\(J(t)[-E(t)/2]^2\). Overall Taylor subtraction sets only physical
external momenta to zero; the host loop remains integrated.

\section*{Three-dimensional density}
Let \(t=[s/(1-s)]^2\), with \(s,z,\theta\in(0,1)\). Choose
\[
 q\cdot p_1=\sqrt{tz}\cos(\pi\theta),\qquad
 q\cdot p_2=\frac{\sqrt{tz}}2[-\cos(\pi\theta)+\sqrt3\sin(\pi\theta)].
\]
The common radial factor is
\[
 W(s)=\frac{2}{s(1-s)}\left(\frac{t}{1+t}\right)^3,
\]
and the orientation density is \(1-z\). Normalize the three
remaining host ratios at zero physical momentum. If \(F(\lambda)\)
is their product with \(J,T_1,T_2\), with only physical momenta
scaled by \(\lambda\), define
\[
 A=\frac{F(1)+F(-1)}2-F(0),\qquad C=\frac{F''(0)}2.
\]
The direct and controlled densities are respectively
\[
 W(1-z)A,\qquad W\{(1-z)(A-C)+\tfrac12\langle C\rangle\}.
\]
The half is required because \(\int_0^1(1-z)\,\mathrm dz=1/2\).
The six-dimensional mean uses
\(\langle(q\cdot p_a)(q\cdot p_b)\rangle=t(p_a\cdot p_b)/6\).
This is a zero-integral control, without a new physical counterterm.

The independent reference keeps all four original host propagators
and uses \(B\), independently differentiated scalar functions,
factorwise Taylor convolution including \(1/2!\), and twelve axial
directions. It does not share the native regrouping or covariance
calculation.

\section*{Whole-axis bound}

bounds give \(J,T_i\leq L\) in absolute value, first physical
derivatives bounded by \(2L/\sqrt t\), and second derivatives
bounded by \(40L/t,16L/t,16L/t\), respectively.
Their product therefore has second derivative bounded by
\(96L^3/t\). This is below the previous mixed-family constant
\(120\), so the radial majorant
\[
 3672\,\frac{\log^3(1+t)}{t^2}
\]
still applies, including the zero-integral control.

For \(0\leq t\leq16\), the bounds for
\((J,J',J'')\) are \((1,10,502)\), and for each triangle
\((2,2,5)\). The three-child product has bounds \((4,48,2196)\).

 1206\cdot4+2\cdot30\cdot48+2196=9900
\]
times \((1+t)^3\), hence the compact radial bound \(7425t^2\).
These estimates establish absolute integrability, not numerical
integration accuracy.
