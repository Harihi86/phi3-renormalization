This note describes candidates 74 and 75. It establishes algebraic
representations and their tested implementation, not whole-integral precision.
Original graphs, recursive BPH programs, forest signs and evidence are retained.

\section{Conventions and the original forest}
The loop measure is $\mathrm{d}^6k/\pi^3$ per loop. These original integral
programs contain no coupling or symmetry factor. The squared mass and physical
invariants are one. For each actual forest $w$, its routed Gaussian matrix gives
$U_w=\det A_w$ and $F_w=U_w h_w$ by the Schur complement.
At four loops, $U_w$ and $F_w$ have parameter degrees four and five.

Internal subgraphs in the present scope require three-point Taylor degree zero.
Their own boundary momenta, including other loop momenta when present, are
projected only in their subgraph factors. Their routes are obtained by
recompiling BPH from each original graph. Incompatible overlapping subgraphs
never occur together in a forest. A powerset of all subgraphs is therefore
not a valid replacement for the recursive program.

\section{Exact common-scale integration}
Write the Schwinger parameters as $s\alpha_e$ and set
$M=\sum_e\alpha_e>0$, $h_w=F_w/U_w>0$ and $r_w=h_w/M$.
The four Gaussian integrals contribute $s^{-12}U_w^{-3}$.
The radial Jacobian is $s^{n-1}$, where $n$ is the number of edges.

For $n=12$, the overall three-point Taylor projector gives
\begin{align}
 \int_0^\infty \frac{e^{-Ms}(e^{-h_ws}-1)}{s}\dd s
 &=-\log(1+r_w),\\
 f_w^{(3)}(\alpha)&=-\operatorname{sgn}(w)U_w^{-3}\log(1+r_w).
\end{align}
For $n=11$, the overall two-point projector has total degree two
in the external momentum vector. Since $h_w$ is quadratic in that vector,
\begin{align}
 J_2(M,h)&=\int_0^\infty
   \frac{e^{-Ms}(e^{-hs}-1+hs)}{s^2}\dd s\\
 &=\int_0^h\frac{h-t}{M+t}\dd t
 =(M+h)\log(1+h/M)-h.
\end{align}
The middle equality follows by writing
$e^{-hs}-1+hs=s^2\int_0^h(h-t)e^{-st}\dd t$; its integrand is nonnegative.
Define
\[
 \ph(x)=(1+x)\log(1+x)-x,\qquad x>-1.
\]
Then
\[
 f_w^{(2)}(\alpha)=\operatorname{sgn}(w)
       \frac{M}{U_w^3}\ph(r_w).
\]
No extra factorial or further subtraction quadratic in $p^2$ is introduced.
The projective forest sum is retained before integration over parameter faces.

\section{Rational differences before transcendental evaluation}
Pair an actual forest state $b$ with a state $z$ obtained by adding
one compatible Taylor subgraph. Their signs are opposite. Put
$s_b=\operatorname{sgn}(b)$ and
\[
 d=r_b-r_z,\qquad x=\frac{d}{1+r_z}>-1.
\]
For the three-point function the exact pair is
\[
 s_b\left[
 \left(\frac1{U_z^3}-\frac1{U_b^3}\right)\log(1+r_z)
 -\frac{\log(1+x)}{U_b^3}\right].
\]
For the two-point function use the exact identity
\[
 \ph(r_b)-\ph(r_z)=d\log(1+r_z)+(1+r_z)\ph(x).
\]
The pair becomes
\[
 s_b M\left[
 \left(\frac1{U_b^3}-\frac1{U_z^3}\right)\ph(r_z)
 +\frac{d\log(1+r_z)}{U_b^3}
 +\frac{(1+r_z)\ph(x)}{U_b^3}\right].
\]
Every coefficient and argument difference is computed as an exact rational.
Arb then encloses the complete sum, with an attained-accuracy check.

Candidate 74 pairs the innermost triangle toggle. Candidate 75 constructs
a deterministic maximum matching on actual opposite-parity forest toggles.
It retains unmatched states using $f_w^{(2)}$.
In particular, L4E2G44 has three forests: one pair and one unmatched state.
Deleting the latter is rejected by the exact partition check.

\section{Projective measure and complete order probabilities}
The full densities have degree $-n$, with $n=12$ or $11$ respectively.
Let $x_j=1$ and $0<x_i<1$ for $i\ne j$, and set
$\alpha_i=x_i/\sum_e x_e$. The simplex Jacobian is $(\sum_e x_e)^{-n}$,
which cancels the density's homogeneous scaling. Thus all $n$ maximum charts
are summed, without division by $n$.

Within a chart, an order $\pi$ and $n-1$ ratios give
\[
 x_{\pi_0}=1,\qquad
 x_{\pi_j}=\prod_{\ell=1}^j t_\ell,\qquad
 J=\prod_{j=1}^{n-1} t_j^{\,n-j-1}\frac{\dd t_j}{\dd u_j}.
\]
For each nonempty remaining set $S$, choose the next edge $e$ with probability
\[
 p(e\mid S)=\frac{W(S\setminus\{e\})}
                  {\sum_{f\in S}W(S\setminus\{f\})}>0.
\]
The integer weights obey an exact subset recurrence. Exact probability
propagation proves total path mass one and counts all $n!$ paths.
This uses $2^n-1$ nonempty subsets, not explicit enumeration of all permutations.
The estimator weight is $J/p(\pi)$.
Averaging $n$ translated selectors contributes $1/n$ only for that average;
it is not a second division of the chart sum.

The proposal face scores are heuristics. They do not prove absolute
integrability, variance bounds or rigorous quadrature errors.
